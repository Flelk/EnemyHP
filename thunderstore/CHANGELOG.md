# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Smoother health bars: the fill and HP number ease to the new value, the colour blends, and the bar glides with the enemy instead of stepping.
- Enemy names now use the game's own display names, in your language (e.g. Pony instead of Snatcher).

## 1.0.0
- Enemy health bars (aim or recent hit), floating damage numbers, kill popups.
