# Changelog

## 2.0.0
- No more health bars or damage numbers on the Skinwalker, so they can't give away its disguise.

## 1.2.0
- New health bar: larger, with a black frame and slightly rounded corners. HP sits above it on the left, the name on the right.
- Bars and damage numbers no longer show through walls, even right after a hit.

## 1.1.0
- Smoother health bars: the fill and HP number ease to the new value, the colour blends, and the bar glides with the enemy instead of stepping.
- Enemy names now use the game's own display names, in your language (e.g. Pony instead of Snatcher).

## 1.0.0
- Enemy health bars (aim or recent hit), floating damage numbers, kill popups.
